# Pre-registration: promoting the TLR4 cascade into T0

**Written 2026-10-19, BEFORE any tier is changed and BEFORE any retrain.**

What is already known at the time of writing, and is therefore **not** blinded — this is the whole
reason the document exists:

* PAR2_29 Fig 3N has been read: pSTING falls ~4.5 → ~1.3 (and step 31b finds that panel
  confounded — the only arm that edits PAR2 also carries albumin).
* The collaborators' dynasore RNA-seq has been read: Sting1 and Bak1 both log2FC ≈ −1.
* The current model predicts ΔSting1 **+0.0060** on PAR2 KO (signal node) and **+0.0119** under a
  simulated dynasore cut — both UP, both contradicting those readouts at the activity level.

So any change that moves ΔSting1 downward will look like a success, and **cannot be allowed to
count as one.** The rules below fix what this promotion is for, what would make it wrong, and what
each outcome is permitted to mean — all before the tree is touched.

## 1. Why this promotion is proposed — and the justification may not reference STING

Three reasons, none of which is the Sting1 direction. If none of these had been true, the
promotion would not be proposed, whatever it does to ΔSting1.

1. **LPS enters the model at Tlr4, and every one of Tlr4's out-edges is T1.**
   `config.step13.contexts.lps = [Tlr4, Cd14]`. `Tlr4` has **no T0 in-edges**, and
   `Tlr4 → {Tirap, Myd88, Ticam1, Ticam2, Nfkb1, Mapk14, Csf3}` are **all T1**. The stimulus that
   defines the entire experimental context enters through the unprivileged layer.
2. **The dynasore simulation cannot work without it.** Measured (step 32): cutting
   `Tlr4 → Ticam1/Ticam2` at T1 moves `Ticam1` **−0.0462** but only `Irf3` −0.0045,
   `Ifnb1` −0.0074, `Sting1` −0.0012. The perturbation is swallowed within two hops. Dynasore's
   defining action is blocking **dynamin-dependent TLR4 endocytosis → TRIF**, so if that branch
   cannot carry a perturbation, the experiment cannot be simulated at all.
3. **TLR4/NF-κB is the axis of the study being modelled.** PAR2_29's stated thesis is
   TLR4/NF-κB/ERK/STING crosstalk, and its Figure 2F shows PAR2/albumin colocalising with
   **endosomal** TLR4. Four of the ten scored holdout observations are TLR4-arm outputs.

**Not a reason, and recorded as excluded:** that the TRIF cut moves `Sting1` in the observed
(negative) direction. It does — **−0.0012** at T1, and promotion will amplify it. That is an
anticipated consequence, written down here in advance precisely so it cannot later be presented
as a discovery.

## 3. The side effect that must be declared BEFORE it is observed

T0 edges initialise at **1.0 / (T0 fan-in)**. Of the 19 targets, all but one currently have **no**
T0 in-edges, so they take 1.0 (or share within a block) and dilute nothing.

**The exception is `Tbk1`, and it matters.** Its only current T0 in-edge is **`Sting1 → Tbk1`, at
weight 1.0**. Adding `Traf3 → Tbk1` and `Ticam1 → Tbk1` takes Tbk1's T0 fan-in from 1 to 3:

> **`Sting1 → Tbk1` falls 1.0 → 0.333, a 3× cut to STING's own output arm.**

So this promotion **weakens the STING → TBK1 → IRF3 → IFNB1 route as a side effect**, and `Ifnb1`
is a **scored holdout**. Any change in the Ifnb1 prediction after this promotion is attributable
to that dilution and **may not be reported as an improvement in the model's STING biology.** If
the cost is judged unacceptable, the stated alternative — decided now, not later — is to promote
Block D without `Traf3 → Tbk1` and `Ticam1 → Tbk1`, accepting that the TRIF branch then terminates
at `Ticam1` and the dynasore simulation is correspondingly weaker.

## 5. Predictions, committed in advance

Stated with their uncertainty. Being wrong here costs nothing; writing them afterwards would cost
the experiment.

1. **Dynasore ΔSting1 (signal) flips negative.** The TRIF arm contributes −0.0012 at T1; at T0 the
   per-hop weight rises ~0.046 → ~0.33–1.0, plausibly 10–100× over the Tlr4 → Ticam1 → Tbk1 chain,
   against the endosomal-ERK arm's +0.0132. **Predicted range −0.05 to +0.005** — wide, and the
   sign is genuinely uncertain.
2. **PAR2-KO ΔSting1 (signal) falls but stays positive**, predicted **0.000 to +0.006**. cGAS still
   supplies 77 % of Sting1's up side through a T0 edge this promotion does not touch.
3. **ΔIfnb1 falls** (from the `Sting1 → Tbk1` dilution in §3), and this is a **cost**, not a result.
4. **ΔMapk1 is essentially unchanged**, so the `transmit_min = 0.002` eligibility guard still passes.
5. **The four TLR4-arm holdouts (Tlr4, Gsdmd, Il1b, Il6) get larger in magnitude.** They are
   currently near zero (−0.0017, −0.0001, −0.0090, −0.0138). See §6 — this is circular and does
   not count.

## 6. What each outcome is permitted to mean

| outcome | permitted reading |
|---|---|
| **Dynasore ΔSting1 turns negative** | The model reproduces the collaborators' direction **at the activity level** under a mechanism (TRIF loss) that is independent of the mtDNA → cGAS arm. **This is NOT validation** — the data was read first. It is reported as *exploratory model revision*, and the dynasore RNA-seq may not be quoted as confirming it. It would mean the earlier conclusion ("every repair avenue is closed") was premature, and the ERK ⊣ BCL2 arm is not the only thing that could carry this. |
| **Dynasore ΔSting1 stays positive** | The TLR4 branch, even privileged, cannot outweigh the ERK ⊣ BCL2 → mtDNA → cGAS arm. The step-30 structural finding stands and strengthens: one arm, counted six times, dominates everything. Report the falsification. |
| **PAR2-KO ΔSting1 turns negative** | **Treat with suspicion, not relief.** No edge in §2 lies on the PAR2 → STING route; if promoting the LPS arm flips the PAR2 prediction, the most likely explanation is that the result is weight-redistribution artefact, not biology. Required follow-up before any write-up: the step-27 contribution table on Sting1, to show which in-edge actually changed. |
| **Holdout score rises** | Does **not** validate the promotion. Four of the ten scored observations are TLR4-arm outputs directly downstream of promoted edges, so improvement there is near-circular by construction. The enlarged score is reported with that caveat attached, prominently. |
| **Holdout score falls** | A real cost, reported as such. It does not by itself revert the promotion — §1's justification is structural, not score-based — but it must appear in the write-up next to the benefit. |
| **ΔIfnb1 degrades** | Attributed to the `Sting1 → Tbk1` dilution declared in §3. If the degradation is large, take the §3 fallback (drop the two `→ Tbk1` edges) and re-run **once**. Not an open search over edge subsets. |

## 7. Procedure, and what would make the change wrong

1. **Rebuild with an explicit diff.** Tag `step33_tlr4_t0`. The diff must show **exactly 19 tier
   changes, T1 → T0, and nothing else**; step06 output unchanged. Any other change means the
   promotion did something unintended and is reverted before anything is measured.
2. **Retrain.** `python scripts/run_step23_retrain.py`. Checkpoints go stale on any tier change;
   quoting numbers from stale checkpoints is how the step-24 crash and the step-22 weight error
   happened.
3. **Re-run both simulations**: PAR2 KO (`run_step31_mrna_modality.py`) and dynasore
   (`run_step32_dynasore.py`), both at 5 seeds, reporting signal **and** mRNA for each.
4. **Report every number in §4 side by side, before and after**, including the ones that got worse.

**Fix a stale artifact first.** `outputs/step04_edges.csv.gz` still carries `Cgas → Sting1` at
**T0**, which step 22 demoted to T1; the live model correctly has 76 T0 edges while that file has
77. It was found during this preparation after it had already been used for tier lookups. It must
be regenerated, or every downstream consumer audited, before the rebuild diff in step 1 can be
trusted — a diff computed against a stale baseline proves nothing.

## 7b. ADDENDUM 2026-10-20: the two-arm hypothesis, and the context-dependence prediction

Added **after** PAR2_26 was read in full and **before** the promotion is applied. PAR2_26's Fig 4H
measures pERK and pSTING **in the same 4T1 lysate**: ERK **down**, STING **up**. PAR2_29's Fig 3
measures both in Raw264.7: ERK **down**, STING **down**. ERK falls in both, so ERK's sign cannot
explain the divergence.

**Hypothesis (H1), stated before testing:** `Tlr4` **positively supports** `Sting1`, and is engaged
in macrophages but not materially in 4T1. Two arms then compete:
* ERK ⊣ BCL2 → pore → mtDNA → cGAS → **STING up** (dominates without TLR4 — PAR2_26),
* TLR4 → TRIF → TBK1 → **STING down when TLR4 falls** (dominates in macrophages — PAR2_29, dynasore).

Note this is the **opposite** TLR4 sign from the one first proposed in discussion ("TLR4 drops
Sting1"), and it requires **no sign change anywhere in the model** — only that the TLR4 arm be able
to carry signal.

**Measured before promotion** (R4, 5 seeds, PAR2 KO), establishing that the model cannot currently
express H1 at all:

| context | ΔSting1 | seeds up |
|---|---|---|
| LPS, `[Tlr4, Cd14]` driven (macrophage) | **+0.00596** | 5/5 |
| no TLR4 input (4T1-like) | **+0.00591** | 5/5 |
| `Cd14` only | +0.00596 | 5/5 |

Removing the entire LPS/TLR4 input moves ΔSting1 by **0.8 %**.

**Predictions registered now, in addition to §5:**

6. **After promotion, the TLR4-on and TLR4-off contexts diverge by at least 10×** in ΔSting1.
   This is the core H1 prediction. If they still differ by <2×, **H1 is not supported by the
   model** regardless of what the absolute sign does, and that must be reported.
7. **The sign flips with context**: ΔSting1 **negative** with TLR4 driven, **positive** without.
   Predicted, uncertain. A flip in *both* contexts together would falsify H1 as a *context*
   explanation even while improving agreement with PAR2_29 — and would then be reported as a
   plain prior change, not as resolving the 26/29 contradiction.
8. **ΔTlr4 under PAR2 KO grows** from its current −0.0017 (it is ~0 now).

**What H1 rests on, and what would break it.** The load-bearing assumption is that **4T1 does not
materially engage TLR4**. This has NOT been verified — 4T1 cells do express some TLR4 in the
literature, and PAR2_26's Fig 4H x-axis includes an LPS+2f arm for the 4T1 cells, although the
paper's design applies LPS+2f to the **macrophage** compartment and transfers conditioned medium
(§2.11–2.12). **Check before the write-up relies on H1**: TLR4 expression/responsiveness in 4T1
under these conditions, ideally from the collaborators or from the paper's own data. If 4T1 does
engage TLR4 comparably to Raw264.7, **H1 fails** and the 26/29 divergence needs a different
explanation — most likely the paracrine-vs-cell-autonomous distinction already on record.

**This addendum does not license quoting either paper as validation.** H1 was constructed after
reading both. Its one genuinely independent content is prediction 6 — the *magnitude of context
dependence* — which no observation was fitted to.

## 7c. ADDENDUM 2026-10-20: the three simulation protocols, fixed before the promotion runs

Each study is a **different perturbation**, and conflating them is what produced the apparent
contradiction. Fixed here so the setups cannot be chosen after seeing the answers.

### Simulation A — PAR2_26 (4T1 tumour cell). The context control.

* context: **no TLR4/CD14 input**
* perturbation: `F2rl1` KO, κ = 0.95 — **the only one.** This is the pure PAR2 KO.
* also run with the LPS context on, as the sensitivity check on H1's assumption (§7b).
* current value: ΔSting1 **+0.0059 UP**, which **already agrees** with Fig 4H.

A must stay UP after the promotion. **If the promotion flips A negative, it has broken the one
context the model was getting right**, and that is a cost, not a success.

### Simulation B — PAR2_29 (macrophage, albumin-delivered editing). Two perturbations, not one.

* context: **LPS**, `[Tlr4, Cd14]` driven
* perturbation 1: `F2rl1` KO, κ = 0.95 — the PAR2 editing
* perturbation 2: **`Tlr4` knockdown, κ_TLR4** — this represents the **albumin carrier**, which
  PAR2_29's own conclusion says "colocalize[s] with endosomal TLR4 and subsequently inhibited
  TLR4 downstream signalling". It is **not** part of PAR2 biology and must never be described as
  such.

**κ_TLR4 is a free knob, so it is pinned to measured numbers and reported as a curve, not a
point.** Run κ_TLR4 over **0 → 0.95** and report the whole dose-response, with the paper's three
measured reductions marked on it:

| anchor | source |
|---|---|
| **0.40** | albumin alone, in vitro |
| **0.60** | hAlbumin/cgPAR2, in vitro Raw264.7 — **the arm Fig 3N shows**, so this is the primary comparison |
| **0.77** | hAlbumin/cgPAR2, in vivo lung |

Reporting the curve is what prevents this from being a fit: if ΔSting1 only turns negative at
κ_TLR4 far above 0.60, **the explanation fails at the measured dose** and that must be said.

> **CIRCULARITY, declared now and consequential.** `PAR2_29_C1` contains a **`Tlr4` observation
> currently scored as one of the eight successes.** Under Simulation B, TLR4 going down is an
> **imposed input**, not a prediction. **That observation must be dropped from the holdout score
> whenever Simulation B is the setup**, and the headline restated — it cannot be both a
> perturbation and a prediction. The same applies to anything downstream that is scored purely
> because TLR4 fell.

**Modality note**: the ~60 % is a reduction in TLR4 **protein expression**; the model's `Tlr4`
node is **activity**. The mapping is declared as an approximation, not an identity.

### Simulation C — dynasore (macrophage, NO PAR2 editing)

* context: **LPS**; **no `F2rl1` KO** — dynasore is applied to an otherwise intact system
* perturbation: **edge cuts, NOT node knockouts.** Two groups:
  1. endosomal ERK scaffold — `Arrb1/Arrb2 → {Raf1, Map2k1, Map2k2, Mapk1, Mapk3}` (10 edges)
  2. endosomal TLR4 → TRIF — `Tlr4 → Ticam1`, `Tlr4 → Ticam2` (2 edges, **Block D only**)
* **kept intact and this is the point**: `F2rl1 → Arrb1/2` (β-arrestin is still recruited at the
  membrane, so receptor desensitisation survives), `Arrb1 → Src`, and **Blocks A/B/C — the MyD88
  branch is membrane-proximal and dynamin-INDEPENDENT, so dynasore does not touch it.**

**Why not node KOs** (asked directly, answered here): an `Arrb1` node KO also deletes
desensitisation — `F2rl1` rises **+0.0247** under node KO against **+0.0015** under the edge cut —
and deletes the `Arrb1 → Src` arm. A `Tlr4` node KO would delete MyD88 signalling, which dynasore
leaves running. Both would simulate a far broader lesion than the drug.

* readout: **mRNA is primary** (the collaborators' data is RNA-seq); signal node reported beside it.

**The two arms oppose each other in C, which is what makes it a real test**: cutting endosomal ERK
pushes Sting1 **up** (ERK ⊣ BCL2 → mtDNA → cGAS, currently +0.0132), cutting TRIF pushes it
**down** (currently −0.0012). Whether the net turns negative after the promotion is exactly the
open question, and it is not knowable in advance.

### Registered predictions for A/B/C

9. **A stays UP** (no TLR4 to engage). Breaking this is a cost.
10. **B turns DOWN at or below κ_TLR4 = 0.60.** If it needs κ_TLR4 > 0.77 — beyond the largest
    measured reduction — the explanation is reported as **not supported at the measured dose**.
11. **C turns DOWN in mRNA** (it is already −0.0049) and **the signal node moves down
    substantially from +0.0119**, flipping or not. If C stays firmly positive while B turns
    negative, the TLR4 arm is carrying B through the imposed knockdown rather than through
    genuine TRIF transmission — a warning sign, and it must be checked with the step-27
    contribution table on Sting1 before anything is claimed.

## 8. What this does not license

Promoting the TLR4 cascade does not make the PAR2 → STING prediction tested. It changes the prior,
and the prior is what the project's central claim rests on. **No holdout score, before or after,
contains a Sting1 observation**, and PAR2_29's pSTING panel has been read and is additionally
confounded (step 31b). If the direction flips, the honest statement is "the model was revised
after seeing the data, and the revision is reported as exploratory" — not "the model predicts it."

The defensible independent support remains what it was before this document: the **T0+T1
enumeration**, which favours Sting1 DOWN (44.3 % of 8 801 paths up) and which was computed without
reference to either study.
